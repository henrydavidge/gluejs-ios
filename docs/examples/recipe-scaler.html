Recipe Scaler Shortcut for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“When I share a recipe, ask how many people I'm cooking for and scale the ingredients.”

What you get

Scaled × 1½
3⅓ cups flour · 1½ cups (3 sticks) butter · 3 eggs

How it works

  1. WhenShare a recipe pageSafari share sheet
  2. Shortcuts gets“6 servings”Ask for input
  3. GlueJSRead the ingredients, rescale every amountFractions and ranges too
  4. ThenShow result
Steps
Share a recipe page or selected text. GlueJS reads the page's machine-readable ingredient list (most recipe sites include one for search engines) and rescales every amount.
Connects to
The recipe website you shared, to read the page.
You'll be asked
Once: allow the shortcut to share Safari items with GlueJS.
Keys & cost
None.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: When I share a recipe, ask how many people I'm cooking for and scale the ingredients.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-recipe-scaler --from recipe-scaler
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-recipe-scaler

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
