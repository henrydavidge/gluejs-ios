Stock Portfolio Widget & Price Alerts for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Show what my shares are worth and my total gain, and alert me if anything moves 3% or NVDA drops below $120.”

What you get

🔔 MSFT −4.2% today
🔔 NVDA is below $120: now $117.20
$11,537, −$53 (−0.5%) today

How it works

  1. WhenDaily automationWidget also refreshes every 30 min
  2. Shortcuts getsHoldings & alertsText in the shortcut
  3. GlueJSFetch quotes, add up value and gain, check alertsFinnhub
  4. ThenWidget + alertsEach alert once a day
Steps
You list holdings (“AAPL 10 @ 150”) and alerts in the shortcut. Apple's Stocks app doesn't track shares, cost or alerts.
Connects to
Finnhub stock quotes.
Leaves your phone
Ticker symbols only, never how many shares you own.
Keys & cost
A free Finnhub key, saved in your iCloud Keychain by a one-tap setup shortcut; it can only be sent to finnhub.io.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Show what my shares are worth and my total gain, and alert me if anything moves 3% or NVDA drops below $120.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-stock-portfolio-widget --from stock-portfolio
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-stock-portfolio-widget

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
