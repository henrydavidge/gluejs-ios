Northern Lights (Aurora) Alert for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Tell me when the northern lights might be visible from my town — but only if it's dark and clear.”

What you get

🌌 Aurora possible now
18% chance to the north, Kp 5.3, 10% cloud cover.

How it works

  1. WhenEvery evening hourTime automations
  2. GlueJSAurora + Kp forecast, clouds, darknessNOAA, Open-Meteo
  3. ThenNotify only on good nightsAt most once a night
  4. ThenWidgetRefreshes hourly
Steps
Checks NOAA's aurora forecast for the sky between you and the pole, geomagnetic activity, and local cloud cover and darkness. Silent unless all three line up.
Connects to
NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center and Open-Meteo.
Leaves your phone
Your town's name, to Open-Meteo.
Keys & cost
None; both are free public services.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Tell me when the northern lights might be visible from my town — but only if it's dark and clear.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-aurora-alert --from aurora-alert
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-aurora-alert

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
