Sourdough Starter Peak Timer Widget for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Tap when I feed my starter, and show me on a widget when it'll peak.”

What you get

🍞 Peak in 3h 10m
Peaks around 5:40 PM · fed 1:16 PM (1:2:2, 70°F)

How it works

  1. WhenHome Screen icon
  2. Shortcuts getsFeed ratio and kitchen temperatureText in the shortcut
  3. GlueJSEstimate the peakBaker's rule of thumb
  4. ThenWidget countdownRefreshes every 30 min
Steps
Estimates rise time from the feeding ratio and kitchen temperature, then counts down: rising, at its peak, past peak, hungry.
Connects to
Nothing. It works offline.
Make it yours
Ask your agent to tune the timings after watching your own starter for a few days.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Tap when I feed my starter, and show me on a widget when it'll peak.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-sourdough-starter-timer --from sourdough-starter
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-sourdough-starter-timer

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
