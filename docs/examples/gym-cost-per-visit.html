Gym Cost-Per-Visit Tracker for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Every time I arrive at the gym, log it and show what each visit is costing me this month.”

What you get

🏋️ 9 visits
$5.00 per visit · last month 6 ($7.50 each)

How it works

  1. WhenArrive at the gymShortcuts automation
  2. Shortcuts getsMembership priceText in the shortcut
  3. GlueJSLog the visit, work out cost per visitStore; ignores repeat arrivals
  4. ThenWidget + notificationSilent on repeat arrivals
Steps
A location automation runs the shortcut when you arrive. GlueJS adds the visit to its Store (arriving twice within 3 hours counts once, because GPS can fire more than once) and divides your membership by this month's visits.
Connects to
Nothing. It works offline.
You'll be asked
Once: allow Shortcuts to use your location for the automation, and allow the shortcut to run GlueJS.
Leaves your phone
Nothing. Your location is only used by iOS to trigger the automation; GlueJS never sees it.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Every time I arrive at the gym, log it and show what each visit is costing me this month.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-gym-cost-per-visit --from gym-visits
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-gym-cost-per-visit

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
