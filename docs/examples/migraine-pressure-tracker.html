Migraine & Barometric Pressure Tracker for iPhone

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Let me log migraines with one tap, and once a month tell me whether they line up with pressure changes — privately.”

What you get

6 of your 9 migraines came after a big pressure drop, which only happens 17% of the time where you live. A drop is forecast for Thursday.

How it works

  1. WhenTap “Migraine”Home Screen or Action button
  2. ThenLog a headacheApple Health
  3. WhenMonthly: “Migraine Patterns”
  4. Shortcuts getsHeadaches, last 90 daysApple Health
  5. GlueJSCompare with 3 months of pressureOpen-Meteo
  6. Apple IntelligenceWrite a short summaryApple Intelligence, on device
  7. ThenShow result
Steps
Headaches are logged in Apple Health, so they're backed up, synced and visible to your doctor. Monthly, GlueJS compares each one with the pressure change in the 24 hours before it, against how pressure usually changes in your town. Apple Intelligence writes it up on the phone.
Connects to
Open-Meteo for pressure; Apple Intelligence on this iPhone.
Leaves your phone
Only your town's name. Your headache history never does.
Needs
An iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Switching the step to Private Cloud Compute or ChatGPT works too, but sends the summary off the phone. Not medical advice.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Let me log migraines with one tap, and once a month tell me whether they line up with pressure changes — privately.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-migraine-pressure-tracker --from migraine-patterns
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-migraine-pressure-tracker

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
