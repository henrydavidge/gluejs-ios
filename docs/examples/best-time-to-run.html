Best Time to Run Today: Calendar + Weather Shortcut

A personal iPhone shortcut that GlueJS makes possible: no app to install beyond GlueJS, built by your AI in a few minutes from one sentence.

What to ask for

“Each morning, find the best hour to run: when I'm free, it's light, dry and not too hot. Show my miles this week too.”

What you get

🏃 6pm–7pm
66°F, 0% rain · 12.4 mi this week

How it works

  1. WhenAlarm stoppedShortcuts automation
  2. Shortcuts getsToday's calendarCalendar
  3. Shortcuts getsThis week's distanceApple Health
  4. Shortcuts getsHourly forecast and sunsetApple Weather
  5. GlueJSFind the best free daylight hourRain, temperature, your calendar
  6. ThenWidget + notification
Steps
Shortcuts reads today's events from Calendar, your walking + running distance from Health, and the hourly forecast and sunset from Apple Weather. GlueJS removes hours you're busy or it's dark, scores the rest on rain and temperature, and adds up this week's distance.
Connects to
Nothing. GlueJS makes no network requests; the forecast comes from Apple Weather.
You'll be asked
Once each: Location (for the forecast), Calendar, Health access to Walking + Running Distance, and to share them with GlueJS.
Leaves your phone
Nothing.

Build it

On a Mac with an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or ChatGPT's Codex, say:

“Use GlueJS (gluejs.sireniallc.com) to make me a shortcut: Each morning, find the best hour to run: when I'm free, it's light, dry and not too hot. Show my miles this week too.”

Or start from this example and change it:

npx gluejs-ios@latest new my-best-time-to-run --from run-window
npx gluejs-ios@latest build my-best-time-to-run

Open the .shortcut file it makes, tap Add Shortcut on your iPhone, and run it once. The full source is on GitHub, and llms.txt has everything your AI needs.
